Challenge Awards
NASA Space Apps recognizes exceptional projects each year through its 10 Global Awards. The winning teams are determined by an executive committee of NASA and Space Agency Partner Subject Matter Experts.

Global Nominees
Following the hackathon, Local Event and Universal Event judges will determine the projects that will advance to Global Judging, known as Global Nominees.

Awards02
Global Finalists
During Global Judging, all Global Nominees will be reviewed by NASA and Space Agency Partner Subject Matter Experts to determine the Global Finalists and Honorable Mentions.

Awards02
Global Winners
The Global Finalists will be reviewed by an executive committee from NASA and Space Agency Partners to determine 10 Global Winners. These judges are agency leaders and executives with a variety of subject matter expertise.
