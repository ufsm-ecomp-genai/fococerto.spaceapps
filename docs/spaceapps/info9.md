Increase event awareness and provide supportive resources to hackathon participants. Submissions to become a NASA Space Apps Collaborator closed on July 28.

WHAT’S A COLLABORATOR?
Each year organizations around the world engage directly with the NASA Space Apps Challenge, a hackathon that brings together coders, scientists, designers, storytellers, makers, builders, and technologists. There are two levels of support from our Collaborators.

Global Collaborators make free tools and resources available to the NASA Space Apps Challenge community to support the creation of NASA Space Apps Challenge projects.
Amplification Collaborators promote the NASA Space Apps Challenge to their communities in order to attract a broader variety of participants to the hackathon.
COLLABORATOR EXPECTATIONS & RESPONSIBILITIES
Collaborators are approved on an individual, rolling basis.

Global Collaborators:

Must provide a resource or tool available to 25 or more countries.
Provide resources or tools that help increase participant productivity in their projects and post-hackathon support.
Once approved, they will receive guidance on how to make resource offerings available for free to the NASA Space Apps Challenge community.
Amplification Collaborators:

Must be able to leverage their networks and communications outreach.
Once approved, they will receive a Participant Recruitment Toolkit that provides language and graphics to spread the word among their communities.
WHY BECOME A COLLABORATOR?
Collaborators are known for expanding the reach and enhancing the available resources of the NASA Space Apps Challenge, a hackathon that inspires interest in Earth and space science and exploration.

ENRICH THE HACKATHON
Support a community of participants in 190+ countries and territories by enhancing their experience with cutting edge tools and resources. NETWORKING OPPORTUNITIES
Collaborators will have the opportunity to link themselves and their organizations to a dynamic environment of open innovation and collaboration. As a Collaborator, connect with a dynamic community of technologists, scientists, and innovators

HOW TO BECOME A COLLABORATOR
Step 01
COMPLETE THE COLLABORATOR FORM
Fill out the form to state your intention to be a Global Collaborator or Amplification Collaborator. Only one submission will be reviewed per person.

Step 02
SUBMISSIONS ARE REVIEWED
The NASA Space Apps GO Team carefully reviews all submissions against evaluation criteria.

Step 03
RECEIVE APPROVAL OR DENIAL
The NASA Space Apps GO Team will reach out to let you know if your submission has been approved or denied.

Step 04
CONNECT WITH THE GO TEAM
A designated member of the GO Team will be in touch with you to share relevant materials and information and to guide you through your touchpoints with the program.
