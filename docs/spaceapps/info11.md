NASA Space Apps Collective (2023-2025)

EMPOWERING KNOWLEDGE SHARING FOR GLOBAL IMPACT
In July 2023, NASA Space Apps launched the Collective to further deepen year-round connections within the global NASA Space Apps community. The Collective concluded in June 2025 after two meaningful years of community driven collaboration, learning, and connection.

You can learn more about the Collective in our blog post, The 2025 NASA Space Apps Collective: Looking Back, Looking Up, and by exploring its legacy and resources.
